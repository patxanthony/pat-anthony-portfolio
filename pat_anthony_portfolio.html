As one of the first creative strategists brought into Owner's paid advertising efforts, I helped build the creative process behind a fast-growing restaurant technology brand. My work spanned concepting, scriptwriting, casting, location scouting, shoot production, directing talent, building contractor crews, supervising multi-camera shoots, and overseeing post-production across a team of editors.

The creative challenge was to make complex restaurant software feel simple, useful, and human to busy restaurant owners. Across Meta, YouTube, CTV, and paid social, the work helped support $5.5M+ in 2025 media spend and contributed to a 58% year-over-year performance lift during a period of major company growth, including Owner's Series C and ~$1B valuation milestone.

Notable work included my AI Game Changer creative, which generated $1M+ in revenue on roughly $380K in spend, along with a range of UGC, product-led, and CTV campaigns that helped establish Owner's paid creative language.

Gundry MD

For Dr. Gundry's BC3 offer, I worked as a producer and editor and created the "Dinner Foods" campaign for their Quiz Funnel. A performance video built around a simple but powerful health insight: the foods people eat every night may be affecting how they feel. The creative challenge was to make the message feel clear, credible, and urgent while still building enough story to hold attention.

The ad became one of the strongest performers of the year, spending roughly $830K at 131% ROI and earning Creative of the Year 2024. When connected to the BC3 quiz funnel, the creative helped drive approximately $1.6M in spend, $2.2M in revenue, 132% ROI, a $126 CAC, and a $162 AOV.

Across the broader quiz effort, BC3 reached approximately $9.7M in spend and $11.5M in revenue, with June-December quiz creatives generating roughly $6.43M in spend and $7.77M in revenue.
